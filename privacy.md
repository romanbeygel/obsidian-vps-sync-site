# Privacy Policy — Obsidian VPS Sync

**Last updated: October 16, 2026**

## Overview

Obsidian VPS Sync is a personal application designed to synchronize an Obsidian knowledge base between Google Drive and a private Ubuntu server.

## Information Accessed

With the user's authorization, the application accesses files and folders in Google Drive, including Markdown notes, attachments, and Obsidian configuration files.

## How Information Is Used

Google Drive data is used solely to synchronize and maintain the user's personal Obsidian knowledge base.

The application may read, create, update, and delete files as necessary for synchronization.

## Data Storage

Files are processed and stored on the developer's private server. Selected versions may also be stored in a private Git repository for backup and recovery.

## Data Sharing

The application does not sell personal data or share Google Drive content with advertisers. Google Drive content is not intentionally made publicly accessible.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Synchronized files and backups are retained until removed by the developer, subject to the retention policies of the storage services used.

## Security

The application uses OAuth authorization for Google Drive access and restricts access to credentials and synchronized files on the private server.

## Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact the developer through the support email provided on the Google OAuth consent screen.
